## The board

Texas A&M RoboMasters needed an embedded platform for robot subsystems without rebuilding the same power, sensing, communication, and programming circuitry for each prototype. I worked on a custom STM32H723 board to bring those functions together.

## Design scope

The board combines a 24 V-to-5 V buck stage, a 5 V-to-3.3 V LDO, an STM32H7 core, an external crystal, an LSM6DSO32R IMU, CAN and UART interfaces, SWD programming, and expanded GPIO.

I created Altium footprints for parts missing from the team library and worked on layout, wiring, soldering, STM32 programming, and integration with the mechanical and software teams. I also supported a CAN breakout board used elsewhere in the robot.
